Good to Know

Questions People Ask Me

Straight answers to the things that matter most before you trust someone with your money.

How do you get paid?
As a distributor, I earn a small ongoing commission (called trail) from the mutual fund company (AMC). It is already built into the scheme and you do not pay me any separate or upfront fee. Because this commission continues only while you stay invested, my interest is aligned with yours: doing well over the long term.
Is my money safe? Where is it actually held?
Your money never sits with me. When you invest, it goes directly to the mutual fund company and is held by them and their registrar (such as CAMS or KFintech). I help you choose, set up and review your investments, but I never take custody of your funds.
What does "AMFI-registered distributor" mean?
It means I am registered with AMFI, the mutual fund industry body, under ARN-346031, and am authorised to distribute mutual funds. Every recommendation I make must follow AMFI's code of conduct: transparent, compliant, and in your interest.
Do you guarantee returns?
No. Mutual funds are market-linked, so returns vary and are never guaranteed. Anyone promising "assured" or "fixed" returns on mutual funds is a warning sign. What I offer is honest guidance, a plan matched to your goals, and the discipline to stay the course.
How much do I need to start?
You can begin a SIP with as little as ₹500 a month in many schemes. It matters far more that you start the habit than how big you start.
Can I change, pause or stop my SIP later?
Yes. SIPs are flexible: you can increase, reduce, pause or stop them, and redeem your money when you need it (subject to any exit load or lock-in, which I will always explain up front).
I already invest on my own. Can you still help?
Absolutely. Many people come to me for a free second opinion on their existing portfolio. I am happy to review what you hold and how it maps to your goals, with no obligation.
